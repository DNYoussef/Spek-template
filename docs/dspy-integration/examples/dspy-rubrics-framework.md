# DSPy Scoring Rubrics Framework for SPEK Agent Communication

## Framework Overview

This comprehensive scoring rubrics framework provides standardized evaluation criteria for DSPy-optimized SPEK agent communication patterns. The framework ensures consistent quality assessment across all communication types while enabling measurable optimization through the DSPy beginner workflow.

## Universal Scoring Principles

### Core Quality Dimensions

All SPEK agent communications are evaluated across five universal dimensions with weighted importance based on communication type:

1. **Clarity & Specificity** - Message comprehensibility and action clarity
2. **Measurability & Evidence** - Quantified metrics and concrete evidence
3. **Strategic Alignment** - Business context and organizational goals
4. **Operational Excellence** - Execution quality and resource efficiency
5. **Learning Integration** - Knowledge capture and process improvement

### Theater Detection Integration

Every scoring rubric includes mandatory theater detection criteria:
- **Concrete Evidence Requirements** - All claims backed by verifiable artifacts
- **Quantified Success Metrics** - Numerical measurements for all achievements
- **Honest Challenge Reporting** - Authentic discussion of blockers and limitations
- **Actionable Next Steps** - Specific, time-bound commitments with accountability
- **Quality Gate Compliance** - Objective validation against established thresholds

## Communication-Specific Rubrics

### 1. Queen → Princess Strategic Directives

**Purpose**: Convert strategic objectives into actionable domain assignments with resource allocation and success metrics.

#### Scoring Matrix

| Criterion | Weight | Measurement Method | Target Score | Quality Gate |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Strategic Clarity** | 25% | Objective comprehensibility and business context (1-10) | ≥9 | CRITICAL |
| **Actionability** | 30% | Task specificity and measurable deliverables (1-10) | ≥8 | CRITICAL |
| **Resource Alignment** | 15% | Resource allocation realism and constraint adherence (1-10) | ≥8 | HIGH |
| **Quality Integration** | 20% | Quality gates definition and measurability (1-10) | ≥9 | CRITICAL |
| **Success Metrics** | 10% | Quantifiable success criteria with validation methods (1-10) | ≥9 | HIGH |

**Overall Target Score**: ≥8.5/10

#### Detailed Evaluation Criteria

**Strategic Clarity (25% weight)**
- **Exceptional (9-10)**: Crystal clear strategic objective with complete business context, obvious domain alignment, market implications understood
- **Good (7-8)**: Clear objective with solid business context, good domain fit, minor context gaps
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Understandable objective but missing business context or unclear domain alignment
- **Poor (1-4)**: Vague objective, poor business context, misaligned domain assignment

**Actionability (30% weight)**
- **Exceptional (9-10)**: All tasks highly specific with concrete deliverables, clear timelines, measurable outcomes, unambiguous execution path
- **Good (7-8)**: Most tasks specific with good deliverables, realistic timelines, mostly measurable outcomes
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some tasks specific but deliverables or timelines unclear, partially measurable
- **Poor (1-4)**: Tasks vague, deliverables poorly defined, timelines unrealistic, unmeasurable outcomes

**Quality Integration (20% weight)**
- **Exceptional (9-10)**: Comprehensive quality gates with specific thresholds, clear checkpoints, automated validation methods
- **Good (7-8)**: Good quality gates with mostly measurable criteria, reasonable checkpoints
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic quality gates but some thresholds unclear or validation methods undefined
- **Poor (1-4)**: Poor or missing quality gates, unmeasurable criteria, no validation plan

#### Theater Detection Markers
- **Evidence Requirements**: All deliverables specify concrete evidence (files, URLs, metrics)
- **Measurement Precision**: Numeric thresholds for all quality criteria
- **Validation Checkpoints**: Specific dates/milestones for verification
- **Escalation Triggers**: Clear conditions requiring Queen intervention
